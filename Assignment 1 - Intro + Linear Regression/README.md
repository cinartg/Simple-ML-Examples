# Assignment 1: Introduction & Simple Linear Regression
---
##  Welcome to the first assignment in the Simple-ML-Examples repo!
### The first assignment will involve fitting a simple neural network with a single linear layer to the equation $3x + 12$. You will then verify your results by checking the values stored within the ```W``` (weight) and ```b``` (bias) to see if they closely match your target equation, which corresponds to ```W``` having a value of 3 and ```b``` having a value of 12.
---
## What you will learn:
### This assignment is designed to teach you the basics of PyTorch and writing your very first PyTorch script. It will teach you how to initialize a "target" set, $Y$, as well as initializing your very first ```nn.Module``` class and layer inside of it, along with the ```forward()``` method. Finally, you will learn how to choose your optimizer for the optimization task, make a prediction, get a loss, compute the gradient, and step through that gradient to ultimately decrease loss and train your model.
